Common NBN Problems We Fix

Slow NBN Speeds

Slow speeds often come from:

  • Network congestion
  • Faulty modem or router
  • Poor internal wiring
  • Wi-Fi interference
  • Old hardware

NBN Dropouts

Dropouts may be caused by:

  • Damaged cabling
  • Pit water damage
  • Loose connectors
  • Faulty NBN equipment
  • Wi-Fi instability

No Internet Connection

Even when Wi-Fi shows “connected”, you may still have no internet due to:

  • Modem faults
  • Incorrect configuration
  • Line issues
  • NBN outages

NBN Installation Problems

Many Sydney homes experience:

  • Delayed activation
  • Incorrect technology setup
  • Poor wiring
  • Weak Wi-Fi coverage
